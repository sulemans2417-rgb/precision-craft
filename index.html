Our Process

The Precision Craft Standard

The finished look gets the attention. The prep work earns it — here's what happens beneath every square foot we lay.

  1. Step 01

    Consultation & Concept

    We visit your Ottawa property, assess the grading, map utilities, and draft a clear layout.

  2. Step 02

    Sub-Base Engineering

    We excavate deep, pack aggregate rigorously, and ensure flawless drainage — the layer no one sees, and the one everything depends on.

  3. Step 03

    Master Craftsmanship

    Every stone is laser-leveled, cuts are seamless, and polymer sand is locked in for decades of structural integrity.
